Happy Birthday Meri Pyari Didi! 🎂🎉

Aaj aapka special din hai aur main chahta tha ki aapko koi aisi cheez bheju jo hamesha yaad rahe. Sach bataun toh hum dono din bhar kitni bhi behas kar lein, TV ke remote, charger ya khane ke peeche kitna bhi jhagad lein, par ghar mein agar aap ek din bhi na dikho toh poora ghar soona-soona lagta hai. Bachpan se lekar aaj tak aapne sirf ek badi behen ka nahi, balki ek best friend, ek guide aur kabhi-kabhi toh doosri mummy ka bhi role nibhaya hai.

Mujhe aaj bhi yaad hai jab bhi mujhse koi galti ho jaati thi aur mummy-papa se daant padne wali hoti thi, toh aap hamesha dhaal bankar mere aage khadi ho jaati thi. Kabhi apni pocket money se mujhe chupke se kuch dila dena, toh kabhi raat ko der tak baithkar meri fizool ki baatein aur tension sunna—yeh sab sirf aap hi kar sakti ho. Main shayad kabhi bolkar nahi dikhata, par main dil se aapki bahut izzat karta hoon aur aapse bahut zyada pyar karta hoon.

Aapne hamesha meri chhoti-chhoti khushiyon ka khayal rakha hai, bina badle mein kuch maange. Aaj aapke birthday par bhagwan se bas yahi dua hai ki aapke saare sapne poore hon, aap zindagi mein itni tarakki karo ki mujhe aapse badi-badi party aur mehenge gifts maangne ka mauka milta rahe! Hamesha aise hi hasti-muskurati raho, kyunki aapki smile se hi ghar mein raunak rehti hai.

Ab kyunki filhaal mera budget thoda tight chal raha hai aur jeb khaali hai, toh mehenge gift ki jagah maine apna dimaag aur mehnat lagakar aapke liye yeh special website banayi hai. Kaam chala lena aur badle mein mujhe acchi si party de dena! Once again, Wish You A Very Happy Birthday Didi!
